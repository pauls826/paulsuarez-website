About

Collaborative, practical, focused on your goals.

My approach

Through telehealth visits, I work with you to understand your concerns and build a treatment plan that fits your life. We decide together what to try, and we keep checking whether it’s working.

Experience

I help adults navigate ADHD and its impact on work, relationships, and everyday routines. We’ll consider your strengths, challenges, and preferences as we build your care plan and review it over time.

Specialties

What I treat

Adult ADHD/ADD

Evaluation and treatment for adults whose attention, follow-through, or time management is getting in the way.

Anxiety disorders

Care for worry, tension, and panic that make daily life feel harder than it should.

Depression

Support for low mood, low energy, and losing interest in things that used to matter.

How visits work

All by telehealth, from wherever you are.

Appointments are by video for adults in New York. Spend part of the year in Florida? I’m licensed there too, so your care can continue while you’re away.

  1. Book online

    Pick a time that works for you through the secure booking page.

  2. Initial evaluation

    60 minutes

    We talk through your concerns, review your psychiatric and medical history, and build a treatment plan together.

  3. Follow-up visits

    30 minutes

    We review symptoms, progress, and medications, and adjust your plan as needed.
